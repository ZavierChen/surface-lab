Reading the model

Coefficients can be positive, negative, or zero. A zero coefficient removes its variable, regardless of the selected exponent. The number fields accept precise values, including values smaller than a slider step.

Classification uses the full equation after normalization by Q. One linear variable plus two same-sign squares gives an elliptic paraboloid; opposite-sign squares give a saddle. Changing an exponent alone does not determine the shape.

Quadrics use analytic geometry. General cubic and quartic surfaces use a cached 29³, 41³, or 57³ sampling grid for F = 0. Very small features may require a smaller plot window and higher quality. General traces are numerical approximations. Transparent surfaces can show WebGL depth-order artifacts.
